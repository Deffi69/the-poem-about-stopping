## The poem

A life spent practising how to hold on — the rope, the rail, a mother's
hand — set against a river that runs its whole life toward the moment
it stops, and is not lost in the stopping, only widened. The poem turns
on the difference between being stopped and stopping: the last act of
agency is the one nobody takes from you.
